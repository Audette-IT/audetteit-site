# What this site collects

> Short version: very little, and nothing is sold or shared.

## The contact form

The form on the [contact page](https://audetteit.com/contact.md) doesn't send anything to a server. When you hit send, it opens your own email app with your name, email, and message filled in — nothing leaves your device until you send that email yourself. Once it arrives, I use it only to reply to you.

## Analytics and cookies

The site uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages get used. **It only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.** If you decline or ignore the banner, nothing from Google Analytics or Tag Manager loads and no analytics cookies are set.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets its own cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_…`) and sends Google things like the pages you view, roughly where you are, and what device and browser you're using. Google handles that under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). It's used only to see how the site is doing. It isn't used for ads, and I don't combine it with anything else.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage (not a cookie), so the banner doesn't ask on every page. You can change it any time with **cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. Declining after accepting clears the analytics cookies.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (like IP addresses) to serve and protect the site. That's covered by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser also makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies; see [Google's Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

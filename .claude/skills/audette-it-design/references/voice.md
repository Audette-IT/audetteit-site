## How it sounds

- **First person singular.** "I", "me", "Michael". Never "we", "our team",
  "our technicians" or "Audette IT is proud to...".
- **Casual and plain.** The words a neighbor would use. "Wifi that reaches the
  back bedroom", not "optimized wireless coverage".
- **Short sentences.** Most are under 15 words. One idea each.
- **Concrete.** Name the actual thing (printer, router, smart TV, the kids'
  iPad), not categories ("devices", "endpoints", "infrastructure").
- **Calm and honest.** Say what happens next. Say no when the answer is no.
  No urgency, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- **Explains rather than sells.** The pitch is "I'll fix it and you'll know why".

## Formats

**Email replies to customers.** Open with their name ("Hi Jane,"). Answer the
question in the first line. Say what happens next (usually: tell me a bit more,
and I'll send a quote). Sign off with "Michael", plus "Audette IT ·
audetteit.com" if it helps. Keep it to a few short paragraphs. If the answer is
no, say it kindly in the first line and offer what Michael *can* do.

Example (someone asks about a cracked iPhone screen):

> Hi Sam,
>
> Sorry, I don't do physical repairs on phones, so I can't fix the screen
> itself. An Apple Store or a repair shop is the place for that.
>
> Once it's fixed, or if you get a new phone, I'm happy to help set it up:
> moving your photos and contacts over, getting your accounts signed in, and
> setting up backups so this is less painful next time.
>
> Michael
> Audette IT · audetteit.com

**Short profiles (Google Business Profile, social bios).** Same voice, first
person, and only the facts above. No keyword stuffing, no emoji, no claims about
experience or reviews. Mention Las Vegas and remote, the everyday list, the
bigger jobs and "quote before any work".

**Flyers and print.** One headline (a `mark` on the key words), a lede, the
two service lists, one line on where and cost, and the email address big.
See "Beyond the web" in `apps.md`.

**UI microcopy in apps.** Same voice, even in buttons and errors: "Add a valid
email so I can reply." Empty states say what to do next: "No open requests.
When someone emails, it'll show up here."
